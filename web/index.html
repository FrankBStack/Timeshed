About

Walking network from OpenStreetMap, schedules from the agency's GTFS feed, routed with an own RAPTOR implementation in Rust. Bands are 5 minutes wide; the slider only filters what was already computed, so it is instant. Changing the time or date re-routes.

Jobs reachable within 45 minutes, walking plus transit, from every inhabited census block in Milwaukee County.

Leaving
How this was made

Each block's value is the mean over departures every 10 minutes across the two-hour window, from the same engine as the Explore tab. Jobs are 2023 LODES workplace counts by block; blocks are 2020 TIGER. The "share of morning" views divide the later value by the morning one.

Hover a block for its numbers. The write-up with figures is in the README.
