    How it works

    Walking network from OpenStreetMap, schedules from the agency's GTFS feed, routed with an own RAPTOR implementation in Rust. Bands are 5 minutes wide; the cutoff only filters what was already computed. Moving the time re-routes.

    Where can the city get to work?

    Jobs reachable within 45 minutes, walking plus transit, from every inhabited census block.

    Leaving
    How this was made

    Each block's value is the mean over departures every 10 minutes across the two-hour window, from the same engine as the Explore tab. Jobs are 2023 LODES workplace counts by block; blocks are 2020 TIGER.

    Hover a block for its numbers. The write-up with figures is in the README.
