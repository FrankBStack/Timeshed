Where can Milwaukee get to work?

Jobs reachable within 45 minutes, walking plus transit, from every inhabited census block in Milwaukee County.

Leaving
How this was made

Travel times come from Timeshed, an own RAPTOR implementation over the MCTS GTFS schedule joined to an OpenStreetMap walking graph. Each block's value is the mean over departures every 10 minutes across the two-hour window. Jobs are 2023 LODES workplace counts by block; blocks are 2020 TIGER.

Hover a block for its numbers. The "night as share of morning" view divides the late-night value by the morning one.
